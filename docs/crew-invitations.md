# Crew invitations

## What to build

A Planner chooses people in **Crew** and taps **Launch & invite 6** once. ORIVOIR sends each person a separate text or email on the Planner’s behalf. The message names the Planner and opens the Venture in a browser. The guest can answer immediately. When the date is locked, the same person can RSVP on the Crew entry that holds their availability. Build this in ORIVOIR with React, TypeScript and Supabase. Paperless Post is a reference for the experience discussed in connection with Zapier. ORIVOIR’s backend calls the delivery providers directly and owns the cards, links, permissions, records and responses.

This is the implementation contract for the [living PRD v0.19](prd.md). The [user stories](user-stories.md) define acceptance criteria for each story ID. The current wizard previews the invitation and saves the Venture locally, with optional Gist sync. Cloud delivery, shared joining and server-enforced capacity are implementation work. Start with [the wizard](../src/ui/NewTrip.tsx), [the store](../src/state/store.tsx) and [the launch guide](venture-launch.md).

## Planner and guest experience

| Moment | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Choose people | In **Crew**, show **Choose from contacts** where the browser supports it. Request only selected contacts and supported name, phone, email and optional photo fields, following a user gesture. Keep **Add by phone or email** available on desktop, on iPhone Safari with its default settings, after denial or cancellation, and for missing contact details. Retain everyone already selected. |
| Review the list | Show one row per person with a photo or initials and the chosen mobile number or email. Let the Planner switch the destination or remove the row. Selecting the same recipient twice creates one invitation. Two people named Maya with distinct contact details stay separate. If two people share a destination, ask for a distinct route or offer the group link so they can identify themselves. Treat phone and email as one person only when explicitly selected as alternatives for that row. |
| Launch | In **Budget & Launch**, preview the same card design guests will open. With six ready, the final action is **Launch & invite 6**. With zero selected, use **Launch Venture** and offer **Share a group link** after launch. Returning from email-code sign-in restores the entire draft, selected people, delivery choices and preview. Only explicit launch submits a batch. On a launched Venture, the equivalent action for added people is **Send N invites**. |
| Delivery exceptions | Review readiness before sending. If five routes work, label the action **Launch & invite 5**. Keep the sixth row visible with **Add email** or **Share personal invite** as appropriate. Reuse its invitation when resolved. Show who needs attention if a delivery fails later. Retry just the affected recipients. Sending directly is one Planner action regardless of the number of ready recipients. |
| Personal invitation | The text or email identifies the Planner, Venture and purpose. A flexible-date card asks for availability with its cutoff. A fixed-date card shows the date or range, any set times and the Venture time zone, with **Count me in** and **Can’t make it** in the browser. Offer a readable message and link wherever rich previews are unavailable. A personal invitation works without name entry, account creation, an email code or app installation. |
| Crew view | For the Planner, show the privately saved name and photo or initials beside each answer. Tap the row for the selected number or email. Other participants can see only the name or photo the invitee chooses to share within their role’s permissions. Show outstanding named invitations separately from active Crew and capacity. Anonymous group-link visitors give no information about who still owes a reply. |
| Date Lock-In | **Lock this time** saves the schedule and closes voting. Offer one **Send date to N** action for active Crew members with an eligible direct route. Use the ordinary **Set a date** card and notification copy, personalized to the same invitation or membership. A group joiner may choose **Text me when [Planner] picks a date** after submitting availability. Group-only members can return through the original group link and their private access. **Share a group link** stays available. Dismissing the send or a delivery failure leaves the date locked and the card ready to send later. |

A notification can read “Maya invited you to Cabin Weekend. Which dates work for you?” Her mom selects Saturday. Maya sees **Mom · Saturday works** beside her contact photo. After the resend, an explicit **Count me in** changes her attendance to **Going**. Until then her attendance is pending.

If all selected routes need attention, keep those people in the draft. Offer **Launch Venture** with clear wording that the invitations are saved for later. The Planner can fix them after launch or share the group link immediately. **Share personal invite** hands off one recipient’s invitation for a private conversation. Show the difference between a message still queued and one the provider has accepted.

## Identity, counts and access

| Link or credential | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Personal invitation | Opaque, revocable bearer link specific to one recipient and one Venture. It identifies the invitation. A forwarded link gives its holder the same invitation scope. A private RSVP link confers neither account identity nor authority over another person. |
| Group link | One forwardable URL per Venture. A new visitor supplies a display name and joins while entry is open. Someone returning with private access resumes their own entry. A generic share always uses this URL, including when invoked from a personal card. |
| Private response access | Issue or restore access for the same member on a successful response. Support return visits and **Manage my response** on another device. Resolve a valid personal return link to the same invitation/member. A signed-in account plus the matching response credential can upgrade an Attendee to Collaborator. |
| Viewer follow invitation | Separate expiring, single-use sign-in action delivered to the Viewer’s own email. **Request to follow** can create/sign in the account and submit one pending request. Planner approval grants redacted access in **Following**. An availability or RSVP link cannot sign someone in as a Viewer. |

- Apply a single atomic join policy to personal and group links. Previewing, sending and opening use zero spots. The first successful availability or fixed-date RSVP submission creates one active Crew membership and consumes one spot under the agreed opt-in policy, including an explicit negative answer. Attendance and availability totals reflect the actual answer. Each later response to that Venture updates the same membership.
- Enforce capacity, cutoff and phase on the server. A full Venture, the voting cutoff or an early date lock closes new entry as applicable. An invitation can still be pending when joining closes. Show the departure screen with **Request to follow**. Already opted-in members can RSVP after lock. Removing a member revokes personal and manage links and excludes their responses. Existing group links can admit a fresh opt-in while joining is open.
- A personal link and group-link session may refer to the same guest. When they present both valid credentials for the same Venture and the personal invitation is unclaimed, let the guest explicitly attach it to their own existing entry. If two claimed identities conflict, identify who the person is responding as and keep the records separate. A matching display name, phone number, email address or contact photo alone supplies no proof of an existing membership.
- Store the Planner’s address-book name, photo and delivery destination privately. Keep them out of the shared roster, link previews, third-party analytics, AI inputs and legacy Gist sync. Keep private tokens out of logs and preview metadata. Use an initials fallback if the picker does not return a photo. The recipient can supply a separate display identity for the Crew.

## Server and data contract

The following names describe responsibilities. Match existing models where equivalent and keep the legacy browser data readable.

Keep provider keys and service-role credentials in Supabase Edge Function secrets. The Vite client uses scoped public credentials for authorized client operations. Contact import, RSVP identity and Viewer sign-in are distinct operations.

| Record | Fields and constraints |
| --- | --- |
| Venture invitation | Stable invitation ID, Venture ID, issuer, nullable linked membership, lifecycle (pending, claimed, revoked), private recipient label, optional private avatar reference, selected delivery channel and destination. Store a comparison key for normalized destinations scoped to that Venture. Keep one intended invitation per recipient and route changes on the same record. |
| Private access | Store digests of high-entropy tokens scoped to a Venture, purpose and invitation or membership, with revocation and an expiry where applicable. The server validates the scope before reading or changing private data. More than one delivered personal card can resolve to the same record. Store any recoverable value needed to deliver a queued link only in protected server-side storage. |
| Membership and responses | One active membership per claimed invitation. Availability and final attendance each reference that membership. Keep the prior availability when an RSVP arrives. Keep response credentials and delivery contacts outside public projections. |
| Delivery intent and attempts | Record the recipient, purpose (availability, fixed-date RSVP), schedule version, channel, unique send operation, status, attempt count and provider message ID where supplied. Distinguish queued, provider-accepted, delivered when confirmed, failed and uncertain. Invitation status, delivery status and RSVP are independent. |
| Messaging permission | For SMS retain the destination, applicable sender, purpose, permission source/time and any withdrawal or suppression. A group opt-in to a date text applies to that Venture update. Check eligibility again at dispatch. |

1. **Launch.** The signed-in Planner sends the draft and selected recipients with a stable operation key. In one database transaction, create or reuse the Venture, invitations and durable delivery intents. A retried launch returns the original result. Clear the private draft only after the server confirms persistence. Expose the group link independently of delivery.
2. **Deliver.** A Supabase Edge Function or worker processes committed intents and calls email or SMS adapters with server-held credentials. One recipient gets one personal URL per message. Authorize and rate-limit sends by Planner and Venture. Claim queued work atomically. Deduplicate per recipient, purpose and schedule version within a send operation. Use provider idempotency where available. Reconcile an uncertain submission before retrying. Track callbacks by provider message ID, verify their authenticity and tolerate duplicate or late callbacks. Send a failed item again without starting another Venture or mailing successful recipients.
3. **Respond.** An HTTPS landing or preview request only reads a minimal public card. On explicit submission, validate the private or group scope, date state, opt-in cutoff and capacity, then claim/create the membership and write the answer in one transaction. Repeated submits and calls from two tabs return the same member. Apply Supabase RLS and return only role-appropriate fields. A Planner’s browser receives updates via Realtime.
4. **Lock and resend.** Atomically record the selected schedule and transition to planning. Build the same fixed-date card used at launch from its committed title, dates, times and time zone. For the same channel, only the recipient link, addressing and required channel wording vary. Prepare the eligible direct list, including opted-in group members who supplied a permitted route. Queue a new fixed-date delivery on the Planner’s send action. Skip removed, unjoined or suppressed recipients and handle a group-only crew through the existing group URL. A delivery callback never changes an RSVP or reopens a vote.

Provide HTTPS invite URLs that work outside a signed-in browser session. The current GitHub Pages hash router renders the prototype. For per-Venture message previews, serve safe card metadata on a public HTTPS route before handing off to the React page. Only show content approved for the invitation preview, with no phone, private avatar, response, or personal token in preview image/metadata URLs. Test link-only SMS as well as clients that render a thumbnail.

Honor the chosen provider’s first-invite permissions, country support, sender registration and opt-outs. A contact-picker grant covers sharing selected device fields. The recipient’s permission to receive an ORIVOIR text is a separate question. Paperless Post’s host terms and geographic coverage describe its own service. Validate ORIVOIR’s provider before enabling a route. The Planner can still deliver eligible invitations and see the remaining names. The product stays free for consumers.
